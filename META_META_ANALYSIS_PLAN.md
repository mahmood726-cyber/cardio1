## Domain Breakdown

### 1. Acute Coronary Syndromes (40 trials, 133,444 patients)

#### A. STEMI Management (15 trials)
- Primary PCI vs fibrinolysis
- Aspiration thrombectomy
- Door-to-balloon optimization
**Key Result**: Primary PCI reduces death/MI by 27% vs fibrinolysis (RR 0.73)

#### B. NSTEMI/Unstable Angina (15 trials)
- Early invasive vs conservative strategy
- Antiplatelet combinations (clopidogrel, ticagrelor, prasugrel)
**Key Result**: Early invasive reduces death/MI by 20% in high-risk patients

#### C. Post-MI Management (10 trials)
- Intensive statin therapy
- DAPT duration after DES
**Key Result**: Intensive statins reduce events by 16%, DAPT optimal at 12 months

---

### 2. Atrial Fibrillation (29 trials, 39,168 patients)

#### A. Rate vs Rhythm Control (8 trials)
**Paradigm Shift**: Early rhythm control (within 1 year) reduces events by 21% (EAST-AFNET 4)

#### B. Catheter Ablation (12 trials)
**Key Result**: Ablation superior to drugs, especially in HF (38% risk reduction in CASTLE-AF)

#### C. Anticoagulation (9 trials)
- Warfarin vs antiplatelet
- DOACs vs warfarin (from earlier datasets)
- Left atrial appendage occlusion
**Key Result**: Anticoagulation reduces stroke by 50-70% vs antiplatelet

---

### 3. Hypertension (35 trials, 291,771 patients)

#### A. Blood Pressure Targets (15 trials)
- Intensive vs standard targets
- Age-specific targets (elderly, very elderly)
**Key Result**: Intensive BP lowering (<120 mmHg) reduces CV events by 24% in high-risk non-diabetics (SPRINT)

#### B. Drug Class Comparisons (20 trials)
- ACE-I, ARB, CCB, diuretics, beta-blockers
- Network meta-analysis possible with 6 drug classes
**Key Result**: All major classes effective; diuretics, ACE-I, CCB preferred for most patients

---

### 4. Heart Failure (60 trials total, split across domains)

#### A. HFrEF Medications (35 trials across multiple datasets)
- Beta-blockers (10 trials): 35% mortality reduction
- ACE-I (6 trials): 23% mortality reduction
- SGLT2i (5 trials): 23% reduction in CV death/HF hosp
- MRA (4 trials): 30% reduction in HF hosp
- ARNI (2 trials): 20% reduction vs ACE-I (PARADIGM-HF)
- Ivabradine (2 trials): 18% reduction if HR ≥70
- CRT (5 trials): 32% reduction in death/HF hosp

**Combined Effect**: Guideline-directed medical therapy + devices reduce mortality by >70%

#### B. HFpEF (15 trials)
- Most therapies failed until recently
**Breakthrough**: EMPEROR-Preserved - SGLT2i first major positive trial (21% benefit)

---

### 5. Lipid Management (29 trials, 356,239 patients)

#### A. Statins (19 trials including dose-response dataset)
- Primary prevention: 25% reduction in MACE
- Secondary prevention: 20% reduction
- Dose-response: Linear relationship, 17% per 1 mmol/L LDL reduction

#### B. PCSK9 Inhibitors (4 trials)
- 15% additional reduction beyond statins
- LDL reduction of 54-59%

#### C. Non-Statin Therapies (10 trials)
- Ezetimibe: 6% additional benefit (IMPROVE-IT)
- Icosapent ethyl: 25% reduction in high TG patients (REDUCE-IT)
- Fibrates: Benefit in high TG/low HDL subgroup
- Niacin: No benefit when added to statins

### 6. Valvular Heart Disease (25 trials, 12,055 patients)

#### A. Aortic Stenosis - TAVI (5 trials)

#### B. Mitral Regurgitation (10 trials)

- Difference: Patient selection (proportionate vs disproportionate MR)

#### C. Other Valves (10 trials)
- Tricuspid regurgitation (TRILUMINATE, CLASP TR)
- Mitral stenosis (balloon valvuloplasty)

## Potential Meta-Meta-Analyses

### 1. Cross-Domain Heterogeneity Analysis
**Question**: Do treatment effects vary systematically across cardiovascular domains?

**Method**:
- Calculate pooled effect sizes for each domain
- Meta-regression with domain as predictor
- Explore whether age, sex, comorbidities modify effects differently by domain

**Example**: Are beta-blockers more effective in HF than post-MI? Does age modify effects more in AF than HTN?

### 2. Temporal Trends in Treatment Effects
**Question**: Have treatment effects changed over time across cardiovascular medicine?

**Method**:
- Meta-regression with publication year
- Compare effects in trials before/after 2000, 2010, 2020
- Identify domains with improving vs stable vs declining effects

**Example Hypotheses**:
- Older trials show larger effects (publication bias, better comparators now)
- STEMI effects improved with technology (door-to-balloon times)
- HF effects improved with combination therapy

### 3. Risk-Benefit Trade-offs Across Domains
**Question**: Which interventions provide the best balance of efficacy and safety?

**Method**:
- For each domain, calculate:
  - Number Needed to Treat (NNT) for benefit
  - Number Needed to Harm (NNH) for major adverse events
  - Therapeutic index (NNH/NNT)
- Rank interventions across all domains

**Example**: Compare NNT for:
- Statins in primary prevention (NNT ~60-80)
- PCI in STEMI (NNT ~35)
- SGLT2i in HFrEF (NNT ~20-25)
- ICDs (NNT ~18)

### 4. Network Meta-Analysis of Meta-Analyses
**Question**: Among all cardiovascular interventions, which provide the greatest mortality benefit?

**Method**:
- Extract mortality data from all 299 trials
- Group by intervention class
- Network meta-analysis comparing all intervention classes
- Rank by SUCRA scores

**Interventions to Compare**:
- Statins
- Antihypertensives (by class)
- HF therapies (ARNI, SGLT2i, beta-blockers, etc.)
- Antiplatelet agents
- Anticoagulants
- Revascularization (PCI, CABG)
- Device therapies (ICD, CRT, TAVI)

### 5. Subgroup Effects Meta-Meta-Analysis
**Question**: Do treatment effects differ by age, sex, diabetes status across domains?

**Method**:
- Extract subgroup data where available
- Meta-regression with interaction terms
- Identify which patient characteristics consistently modify effects

**Key Subgroups**:
- Age (<65, 65-75, >75)
- Sex (male vs female)
- Diabetes (yes vs no)
- Baseline CV risk (low, intermediate, high)
- Geographic region

### 6. Dose-Response Relationships
**Question**: For which interventions do we see dose-response relationships?

**Already Have**:
- Statins: Linear relationship with LDL reduction
- Antihypertensives: BP lowering dose-response

**Can Add**:
- Beta-blocker dose in HF
- Antiplatelet intensity (aspirin dose, DAPT combinations)
- Anticoagulation intensity

### 7. Combination Therapy Meta-Analysis
**Question**: What is the additive effect of combining evidence-based therapies?

**Method**:
- Model expected effect of combining:
  - Statin + antihypertensive + antiplatelet
  - ARNI + SGLT2i + MRA + beta-blocker in HF
  - Intensive BP + lipid + glucose control in diabetes

**Assumption**: Independent effects (multiplicative model)
**Reality Check**: Compare to trials that tested combinations (ACCORD, ADVANCE)

### 8. Economic Meta-Meta-Analysis
**Question**: Which interventions provide the best value (cost per QALY) across cardiovascular medicine?

**Method**:
- Calculate cost-effectiveness using:
  - Treatment costs
  - NNT from our meta-analyses
  - Quality-adjusted life years (QALYs) from literature
- Compare across domains
- Identify highest-value interventions

### 9. Publication Bias Across Domains
**Question**: Which domains have the most publication bias?

**Method**:
- Egger's test, funnel plots for each domain
- Trim-and-fill analysis
- Selection models (Bartoš 2024 method we implemented)
- Compare extent of bias across domains

### 10. Sample Size and Power Analysis
**Question**: Are recent trials adequately powered? What sample sizes are needed?

**Method**:
- Calculate observed effect sizes by domain
- Determine required sample size for 80% power
- Compare to actual trial sizes
- Identify underpowered domains

## Advanced Statistical Methods Available

We have implemented cutting-edge methods:

### 1. Meta-Regression
- Continuous and categorical covariates
- Multiple predictor models
- R² for heterogeneity explained
- QM/QE statistics

**Application**: Age, baseline risk, duration as effect modifiers

### 2. Dose-Response Meta-Analysis
- Linear, quadratic, restricted cubic splines
- Tests for nonlinearity
- Optimal dose identification

**Application**: BP lowering, LDL reduction, drug doses

### 3. Network Meta-Analysis
- Direct and indirect comparisons
- Consistency checking
- SUCRA rankings
- Pairwise comparison matrices

**Application**: Compare all antihypertensive classes, all HF medications

### 4. Robust Methods
- Hartung-Knapp adjustment
- Outlier detection (Huber weights)
- Influence diagnostics

### 5. Publication Bias Methods
- Egger's test, trim-and-fill
- Selection models (Bartoš et al. 2024)
- P-curve analysis

### 6. Bayesian Methods
- Beta-binomial for rare events
- Hierarchical models for multi-domain analysis
- Prior sensitivity analysis
