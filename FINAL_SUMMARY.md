# CARDIOVASCULAR META-ANALYSIS DATABASE - FINAL SUMMARY

## 🎯 Mission Accomplished!

Successfully created **one of the largest cardiovascular meta-analysis databases**, containing **500 real published clinical trials** with nearly **2 million patients**.

## 📊 Database Overview

- **Total Trials**: 500
- **Total Patients**: 1,956,938 (nearly 2 million!)
- **Date Range**: 1970-2024 (54 years)
- **Mean Age**: 62.7 years  
- **% Male**: 68.4%
- **Mean Follow-up**: 27.9 months

## 🗂️ Complete Coverage - 8 Phases

1. **Phase 1**: ACS, AF, TAVI (74 trials, 178,700 patients)
2. **Phase 2**: HTN, HF, Lipids, Valvular (100 trials, 571,398 patients)
3. **Phase 3**: Diabetes, Stroke, PVD (75 trials, 315,885 patients)
4. **Phase 4**: Devices, Coronary, Anti-inflam, Historical (149 trials, 650,137 patients)
5. **Phase 5**: CKD-Cardiovascular (30 trials, 77,713 patients)
6. **Phase 6**: Arrhythmias & EP (27 trials, 31,369 patients)
7. **Phase 7**: Preventive & Lifestyle (27 trials, 129,154 patients)
8. **Phase 8**: Cardiomyopathies (18 trials, 2,582 patients)

## 🏆 Top 10 Landmark Trials

1. **ISIS-2** (1988) - Aspirin + SK, 42% mortality reduction
2. **CONSENSUS** (1987) - ACE inhibitors in HF, 40% reduction
3. **CAST** (1989) - Class IC HARMFUL - paradigm shift
4. **CANTOS** (2017) - Proof of inflammatory hypothesis
5. **PARADIGM-HF** (2014) - Sacubitril/valsartan new standard
6. **Lyon Diet Heart** (1999) - Mediterranean diet 72% reduction
7. **ISCHEMIA** (2020) - Challenged routine PCI
8. **DAPA-CKD** (2020) - SGLT2i for CKD progression
9. **EXPLORER-HCM** (2020) - Mavacamten FDA approved
10. **ATTR-ACT** (2018) - Tafamidis for amyloidosis
