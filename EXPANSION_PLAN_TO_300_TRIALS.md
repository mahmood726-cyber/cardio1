# Systematic Expansion Plan to 300+ Cardiovascular Trials

## Insights from Similar Projects

### What Leading Projects Do:

1. **Cochrane Heart, Stroke and Circulation**
   - Organized by disease categories: Hypertension, Heart Failure, Stroke, Vascular
   - Comprehensive coverage of all interventions
   - ~480 cardiovascular systematic reviews
   - CENTRAL database: largest controlled trials registry

2. **Living Systematic Reviews** (COVID-NMA, MetaInsight)
   - Continuously updated meta-analyses
   - Interactive platforms for exploring evidence
   - Automated data extraction and analysis

3. **Landmark Trials Databases** (lmtrials.com, ACC/AHA)
   - Curated collections of pivotal trials
   - Organized by clinical domain
   - Annual summaries of key trials

4. **Network Meta-Analysis Platforms**
   - Compare multiple treatments simultaneously
   - Use both direct and indirect evidence
   - Interactive visualization tools

## Data Collection Strategy

### Approach:
1. **Systematic search by domain** (not random collection)
2. **Extract from published meta-analyses** (already validated data)
3. **Use landmark trial lists** (ACC/AHA, ESC guidelines)
4. **Cross-reference with Cochrane** (quality assurance)

### Data Sources:
- Published systematic reviews and meta-analyses
- Trial publications (N Engl J Med, Lancet, JACC, Circulation)
- Clinical trial registries (ClinicalTrials.gov)
- Cochrane Central Register of Controlled Trials (CENTRAL)

### Quality Control:
- Only include RCTs with ≥200 patients
- Prefer trials with published meta-analyses
- Include landmark/guideline-changing trials
- Document all data sources and references
