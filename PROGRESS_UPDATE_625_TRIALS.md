## Key Scientific Contributions

### Paradigm-Shifting Trials Documented:

1. **ISIS-2 (1988)**: Aspirin + streptokinase 42% mortality reduction - foundation of modern STEMI care
2. **CONSENSUS (1987)**: ACE inhibitors 40% mortality reduction in HF - launched RAAS blockade era
3. **4S (1994)**: Statins 30% mortality reduction - established lipid-lowering as cornerstone
4. **CAST (1989)**: Class IC drugs INCREASED mortality 2.5x - paradigm shift in arrhythmia management
5. **CANTOS (2017)**: Anti-inflammatory therapy reduces CV events - proof of inflammatory hypothesis
6. **DAPA-CKD/EMPA-KIDNEY**: SGLT2i now standard for CKD progression
7. **PARADIGM-HF (2014)**: Sacubitril/valsartan 20% mortality reduction - new HF standard
8. **ISCHEMIA (2020)**: Routine invasive strategy NO benefit in stable CAD - challenged practice
9. **ATTR-ACT (2018)**: Tafamidis 30% mortality reduction in cardiac amyloidosis - first effective therapy
10. **EXPLORER-HCM (2020)**: Mavacamten for obstructive HCM - first drug targeting hypercontractility

### Largest Trials Ever Documented:
- ISIS-4: 58,050 patients (early ACE inhibitor post-MI)
- WHI: 48,835 patients (low-fat diet)
- COMMIT-CCS-2: 45,852 patients (beta-blockers in STEMI)
- ISIS-3: 41,299 patients (thrombolytic comparison)
- HPS3-REVEAL: 30,448 patients (anacetrapib CETP inhibitor)
